[ CLARITY & TERMS ]

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take a monthly fee or commission cut of our payments?

No. We believe in 100% asset sovereignty. All collections settle directly into your business bank account. You own your code, databases, and physical hardware outright with zero recurring vendor cuts.

Can our staff still process payments during loadshedding or network outages?

Yes. The Counter Standard runs on an offline-tolerant local network ledger. You can scan member cards, log payments, and print thermal slips on backup battery power even when municipal internet is down.

How does the 30-day performance warranty work?

Payment is split into milestones: 50% mobilization deposit, 25% upon live on-site hardware delivery, and the final 25% balance is only payable after 30 days of verified, error-free counter and reconciliation operations.
