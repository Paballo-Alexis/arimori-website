Can our staff still process payments during loadshedding or network outages?

Yes. The Counter Standard runs on an offline-tolerant local network ledger. You can scan member cards, log payments, and print thermal slips on backup battery power even when municipal internet is down[cite: 1].

How does the 30-day performance warranty work?

Payment is split into milestones: 50% mobilization deposit, 25% upon live on-site hardware delivery, and the final 25% balance is only payable after 30 days of verified, error-free counter and reconciliation operations[cite: 1].
